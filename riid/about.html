About RIID

The official intelligence, scientific security, and federal investigation authority of the Binod World Government.

History

Founded to Protect the Binod World

RIID was formally chartered on 30 June 2026, consolidating the government's previously scattered intelligence, scientific security, and federal investigation functions under a single accountable authority reporting directly to the Office of the President.

The founding charter established RIID's dual structure from the outset: a Public Portal for declassified case material, recruitment, and press releases, and a separate, secure Classified Intelligence Network for authorized personnel handling active operations.

Mission

Our Mandate

To protect the citizens, institutions, and sovereignty of the Binod World through disciplined intelligence work, rigorous investigation, scientific security assessment, and coordinated defense — always accountable to the rule of law and the Office of the President.
