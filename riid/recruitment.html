Become an Agent

RIID recruits citizens from every Member Nation who are willing to serve with discipline, discretion, and loyalty to the Binod World Government.

01
Requirements
Citizenship of a Member Nation in good standing, no unresolved offenses on record, and willingness to undergo a background review.
02
Academy Training
Accepted candidates complete the RIID Academy program covering investigation procedure, intelligence tradecraft, and field conduct.
03
Ethics & Conduct
All agents are bound by the RIID Code of Conduct — accountability to the Office of the President, protection of citizen rights, and strict handling of classified material.
04
Application
Submit an application via the Government Contact page. Shortlisted candidates are contacted directly for the next Academy intake.

Training

The RIID Academy

The Academy trains new agents across all four RIID divisions — Research, Intelligence, Investigation, and Defense — before assignment to a specialization track.

Training covers evidence handling, classification discipline, interviewing technique, threat assessment, and coordinated response procedures.
