Procedure

How to Request Access

  1. File a request. Use the public clearance request form. It is public, so give only your in-world name and reason.
  2. Review. The Office of the President checks who you are and whether you need to know.
  3. Clearance granted. You receive a clearance level and, where needed, the compartment for each record.
  4. Read in ENDERGATE. Sign in to the gateway. Each reading is recorded in the audit log.
